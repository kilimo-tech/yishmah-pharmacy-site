What we do for you

Besides filling your prescription, you can ask us for any of these at the counter or over the phone.

  • Medication counselling

    How to take your medicine, and what not to mix it with.

  • Medication therapy management

    Regular reviews if you take several medicines or have a chronic condition.

  • Blood pressure monitoring and management

    A quick, accurate reading and practical advice on what it means.

  • Blood sugar and glucose monitoring

    Testing and support if you have diabetes or are watching for it.

  • Home and hotel delivery

    We bring your medicine to your home or hotel room in Ukunda and Diani.

More about our services

Staying in Diani and need medicine?

You don't have to leave your hotel. Call or WhatsApp us with what you need, the name of your hotel and your room number, and we will bring it to your room. We deliver discreetly, to homes and hotels across Ukunda and Diani.
