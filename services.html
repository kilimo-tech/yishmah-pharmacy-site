Our services

We fill prescriptions and stock quality, essential medicines. We also give you time with a pharmacist, and we will come to you if you can't come to us.

  • Medication counselling

    One-on-one guidance on how to take your medication correctly, understand interactions, and get the most out of your treatment.

    More about medication counselling

  • Medication therapy management

    Structured, ongoing reviews of your medication regimen. This is particularly valuable for those managing multiple prescriptions or chronic conditions.

    More about medication therapy management

  • Blood pressure monitoring and management

    Quick, accurate readings paired with practical guidance for keeping your numbers in a healthy range.

    More about blood pressure monitoring and management

  • Blood sugar and glucose monitoring

    Convenient testing and support for customers managing or watching for diabetes.

    More about blood sugar and glucose monitoring

  • Home and hotel delivery

    Because Ukunda and Diani are as much about visitors as residents, we deliver medication directly to homes and hotel rooms, discreetly and promptly.

    More about home and hotel delivery
