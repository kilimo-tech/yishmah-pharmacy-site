Who we are

Yishmah Pharmacy Limited sits in Ukunda's healthcare corridor, on the ground floor of Noura Suites, directly opposite Amazon Car Wash and next to Aga Khan Hospital. It was built on a simple conviction: a pharmacy should be the place where healthcare feels human again.

We are more than a counter where prescriptions are filled. We are a community pharmacy, with a team of licensed pharmaceutical professionals who take the time to understand the medicine you need and the life you're living around it. Every consultation, every refill and every late-night delivery to a hotel room in Diani carries the same promise: care that goes beyond the paper the prescription is written on.

Our story

We founded Yishmah to close a gap we saw clearly in Ukunda and along the Diani coastline. The area is growing fast and serves residents, expatriates and a steady flow of travellers, yet it is often underserved by pharmacies that treat medicine as a transaction rather than a relationship. We set out to build a pharmacy that works with the discipline of a hospital dispensary and the warmth of a neighbourhood chemist.

Today that shows up in the details. A pharmacist remembers your regular medication without being asked. You get a blood pressure check with no upsell. A delivery rider gets to your hotel room within the hour.

Our mission

To provide accessible, accurate and compassionate pharmaceutical care to every person who walks through our doors, or calls from theirs, by combining clinical excellence with genuine human attention.

Our vision

To be the most trusted name in community pharmacy along the Kenyan coast, recognised for the depth of our care rather than the size of our shelves.

What we stand for

  • Integrity

    Every product we dispense is genuine and correctly stored. We are open with you about what it does and why you need it.

  • Compassion

    We treat every customer as a person first and a patient second. Nobody here is just a transaction.

  • Accessibility

    Round-the-clock opening and delivery mean quality healthcare is never more than a phone call away.

  • Excellence

    Our team keeps building its clinical knowledge so that we can offer you proper counselling at the counter, not just a quick checkout.
